Sections that hold still while the story moves.

02 · Horizontal tracks

A sideways journey, scrolled the usual way.

One engine moves two blocks. Scroll down: the first track slides left; the second travels right, with a progress bar and its cards lighting up as they reach the middle. Speed, direction and settling on a card are options; on phones the cards swipe sideways or stack.

03 · Full-page slides

One gesture, one scene.

Scroll down onto the scenes: they fill the screen and hold it. From there every wheel turn, swipe or arrow key turns exactly one scene. After the last one, the page scrolls on as usual.

04 · Sticky and reveal

Text that rises into place.

Two Group styles do it with CSS alone. Reveal on scroll lets headings and text settle in as they arrive. Sticky section holds a group still for a stretch of the page: keep scrolling and watch the next panel wait for you.

07 · Cookie-less and discreet

Nothing to declare.

The theme sets no cookies, stores nothing in the browser, tracks no one and calls no third party. It also stops announcing how the site is built.

  • No cookies, no browser storage
  • No tracking, no third-party requests
  • Self-hosted fonts
  • WordPress version and user names kept private
  • XML-RPC and discovery links switched off
