Sections that hold still while the story moves.

04 · Sticky and reveal

Text that rises into place.

Two Group styles do it with CSS alone: Reveal on scroll lets headings and text settle in as they arrive, Sticky section holds a group still for a stretch of the page.

07 · Cookie-less and discreet

Nothing to declare.

The theme sets no cookies, stores nothing in the browser, tracks no one and calls no third party. It also stops announcing how the site is built.

  • No cookies, no browser storage
  • No tracking, no third-party requests
  • Self-hosted fonts
  • WordPress version and user names kept private
  • XML-RPC and discovery links switched off
